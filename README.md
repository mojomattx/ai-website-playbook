# AI Website Playbook

A free, step-by-step method to build a website, or to make the one you already have better.

It's the method MojoSons Media follows on every site.

Each written step has clear rules and a yes or no checklist.

## Who it's for

Anyone building a new website.

Anyone who wants to improve a website they already have.

You can follow it with an AI tool, or on your own with no AI at all.

## Where it comes from

MojoSons Media worked on more than 100 websites in 2026.

That work is built on 10 years in marketing and building websites.

This playbook is what we learned, written down so you can use it too.

## How to use it

### Read it

Read the playbook on [mojosonsmedia.com/playbook](https://mojosonsmedia.com/playbook).

### Let your AI run it

Copy the prompt on that page and paste it into your AI tool.

The prompt tells your AI where this repo is and takes you through the written steps.

If your site already exists, your AI checks it against the checklist of each written step and lists what to fix.

If your site is new, it takes you through the written steps in order.

It also tells your AI to ask for your yes before it changes anything on your live site.

Does your AI tool read code repos on its own?

Point it at this repo and ask it to read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) first.

## The 34 steps

1. [Write your website project plan](steps/01-website-project-plan.md): your goal, your audience, your templates, your costs, and what to build first.
2. [Build your project knowledge base](steps/02-project-knowledge-base.md): one home for your project's facts, rules, decisions, and progress.
3. Write SOPs for every task (coming soon): an SOP is a process made of actions to take, like a checklist, so every task gets done the same way.
4. Write your brand style guide (coming soon): your brand, your voice, and the style rules every page follows.
5. Secure your website and protect visitor data (coming soon): keep your site safe, and collect only the data you need.
6. Make your website fast (coming soon): pages that load quickly on phones and computers.
7. Set up technical SEO (coming soon): the settings that let search engines find and read your pages, and add them to their results.
8. Set up your sitemap, robots.txt and page dates (coming soon): the files that tell search engines which pages exist, where they may go, and when each page changed.
9. Do your keyword research (coming soon): find the searches your pages should answer.
10. Plan your website structure (coming soon): every page your site needs, and how the pages connect.
11. Design for accessibility and for phones first (coming soon): pages everyone can read and use, designed for phones first, then bigger screens.
12. Set up your design system (coming soon): one set of colors, fonts, and building blocks for every page.
13. Plan your navigation and page layout (coming soon): menus and layouts that help visitors find what they need.
14. Brief, write, and approve your website copy (coming soon): the words on every page, from the brief to the final yes.
15. Write like a person, not a machine (coming soon): copy that sounds natural and is easy to read.
16. Format your pages for skimming (coming soon): short paragraphs and clear headings people can scan.
17. Back every fact with a source (coming soon): every fact checked and linked to its source, so readers can trust what you say.
18. Write calls to action, FAQs, and sales copy (coming soon): the words that turn a visitor into a customer.
19. Place your headings and keywords for on-page SEO (coming soon): where your main keyword goes on each page.
20. Write your meta titles and descriptions (coming soon): the title and short text people see in search results.
21. Add schema markup (coming soon): code that tells search engines what each page is about.
22. Link your pages together (coming soon): internal links that help visitors and search engines move through your site.
23. Optimize your images and share cards (coming soon): images that load fast, and the preview people see when your page is shared.
24. Build a multilingual website (coming soon): translate your site, and tell search engines which language each page is in.
25. Build from templates, in the right order (coming soon): put each page together from its template, step by step.
26. Write clean front-end code (coming soon): simple, light code for what runs in your visitor's browser.
27. Review and approve every page (coming soon): nothing goes live without a check and a yes.
28. Run the pre-launch checklist (coming soon): the checks your site passes before it goes public.
29. Publish your site, get it indexed, and watch it (coming soon): put your site live, get it into search results, and catch problems early.
30. Promote your website (coming soon): social media and links that bring visitors.
31. Set up your content creation process (coming soon): a publishing rhythm, and a schedule to refresh older pages.
32. Keep your facts and data up to date (coming soon): prices, dates, and numbers that stay right after you publish.
33. Run an SEO audit (coming soon): check your site's SEO, and list what to fix.
34. Maintain your website and improve how you work (coming soon): regular checks, and every mistake turned into a rule so it doesn't come back.
